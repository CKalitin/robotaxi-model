# Tesla Robotaxi — official milestone timeline

Dated bullets, most confident date first where sources disagree slightly. Compiled from live web
research on 2026-07-22; see `sources.md` for the full source list. All figures are as reported at
the time by the cited source, not verified against a live Tesla-controlled system (Tesla does not
publish one).

- **2024-10-10** — Tesla holds its "We, Robot" event at Warner Bros. Studios in Burbank, CA,
  unveiling the purpose-built two-seat "Cybercab" (no steering wheel/pedals, targeted sub-$30,000
  price) and the "Robovan." Production of Cybercab is framed as starting "before 2027."
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/10/10/elon-musk-hypes-a-30000-tesla-cybercab-robovan-at-robotaxi-event.html),
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/10/teslas-we-robot-event-how-to-recap-the-cybercab-robovan-reveals/)

- **2025-04-22** — On Tesla's Q1 2025 earnings call, management reaffirms a plan to launch a paid
  robotaxi (ride-hailing) pilot in Austin, TX by end of June 2025, using existing Model Y vehicles
  (not Cybercab, which is not yet in production) starting with roughly 10 vehicles.
  [Reuters/aggregated coverage via search](https://www.forbes.com/sites/bradtempleton/2025/04/22/musk-robotaxis-in-austin-need-intervention-every-10000-miles/)

- **2025-05-20/28** — Musk publicly confirms a mid-June Austin target date (reports cite both
  June 12 and June 22 as circulating target dates in this window).
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/20/elon-musk-tesla-robotaxi.html),
  [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-05-28/tesla-targets-june-12-launch-of-robotaxi-service-in-austin)

- **2025-06-22** — Tesla Robotaxi launches publicly (invite-only) in Austin, TX — the first
  commercial Tesla robotaxi service. ~10-20 Model Y vehicles, each with a Tesla employee "safety
  monitor" in the front passenger seat (not the driver's seat); flat promotional fare of $4.20.
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/20/tesla-robotaxi-launch-austin.html),
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/22/tesla-launches-robotaxi-rides-in-austin-with-big-promises-and-unanswered-questions)

- **2025-06-23** — NHTSA says it has contacted Tesla and is gathering information after videos
  surface showing Austin robotaxis driving on the wrong side of the road and braking erratically,
  one day after launch.
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/23/tesla-robotaxi-incidents-caught-on-camera-in-austin-get-nhtsa-concern.html)

- **2025-07-25** — California regulators (CPUC) publicly note that Tesla has NOT applied for the
  autonomous-passenger-service authorization needed to run a true driverless robotaxi in
  California; Tesla is reported to be planning a "friends and family" style car service under its
  existing charter permit instead.
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/25/tesla-plans-friends-and-family-service-in-california.html)

- **2025-07-30** — Tesla Robotaxi launches (invite-only) in the San Francisco Bay Area — its first
  market outside Texas. A human safety driver sits in the driver's seat (not the passenger seat as
  in Austin) because Tesla only holds a CA DMV testing permit with a safety driver, not a
  driverless deployment permit. Geofence spans roughly the Golden Gate Bridge area down through San
  Jose.
  [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/2962/tesla-to-launch-robotaxi-in-the-bay-area-this-weekend),
  [Not a Tesla App (pricing)](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/2974/tesla-launches-robotaxi-in-the-bay-area-with-higher-fares-and-strict-supervision)

- **2025-08 (Aug 2025 onward)** — First of several Austin geofence expansions begins (tracker
  reporting later characterizes the 2026-06-03 expansion as the "fifth" since prior ones in July,
  early August, late August, and late October 2025).
  [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/06/03/tesla-expands-unsupervised-robotaxi-service-to-whole-austin-metro-area/)

- **2025-11-17** — Arizona regulators grant Tesla a statewide Transportation Network Company (TNC)
  ride-hailing permit — but only for safety-driver-supervised operation, not driverless.
  [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/779371/tesla-robotaxi-permit-arizona/),
  [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2025/11/19/tesla-permit-robotaxi-with-safety-driver/)

- **2025-11-26** — Reporting notes Musk has effectively lowered Tesla's originally stated goal of
  500 Austin robotaxis by end-2025 to roughly 60.
  [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2025/11/26/elon-musk-slashes-tesla-robotaxi-fleet-goal-austin/)

- **2025-12-15/22** — Tesla begins testing a small number of Austin robotaxis with no in-car
  safety monitor present at all (as opposed to earlier tests where the monitor simply moved to a
  trailing chase vehicle). Independent tracking (Ethan McKanna's tracker) identifies 32 distinct
  Model Ys in the fleet but observes fewer than 10 operating simultaneously.
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/15/tesla-starts-testing-robotaxis-in-austin-with-no-safety-driver),
  [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2025/12/22/tesla-robotaxi-project-austin-much-smaller-than-musk-claims/)

- **2026-01-22** — Musk announces on X that Tesla has "just started Tesla Robotaxi drives in
  Austin with no safety monitor in the car"; reporting finds the "unsupervised" cars were being
  followed by a trailing chase vehicle containing the safety monitor, i.e., the monitor was moved
  rather than removed for at least some vehicles. Tesla VP of Software Ashok Elluswamy describes it
  as "a few unsupervised vehicles mixed in with the broader robotaxi fleet with safety monitors."
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/22/musk-says-tesla-takes-safety-supervisors-out-of-some-austin-robotaxis.html),
  [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/01/22/tesla-didnt-remove-the-robotaxi-safety-monitor-it-just-moved-them-to-a-trailing-car/)

- **2026-01-28** — Tesla's Q4 2025 earnings / shareholder deck confirms plans to launch Robotaxi in
  Dallas, Houston, Phoenix, Miami, Orlando, Tampa, and Las Vegas in the first half of 2026 (Bay
  Area shown as "safety driver," Austin as "ramping unsupervised"). Tesla states cumulative paid
  Robotaxi miles of roughly 610,000-700,000 since the June 2025 launch (see `ridership.csv`).
  Musk states the combined Austin + Bay Area fleet is "well over 500" vehicles.
  [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-confirms-robotaxi-expansion-plans-new-cities-aggressive-timeline/)

- **2026-02-16** — Independent status check finds Austin robotaxi service availability at just 19%
  over the prior 48 hours, with roughly 42 vehicles counted in Austin (~200 combined Austin + Bay
  Area) — versus the 500-vehicle year-end-2025 target Tesla had originally set.
  [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/02/16/tesla-robotaxi-status-check-8-months-in/)

- **2026-04-18** — Tesla launches unsupervised Robotaxi service in Dallas and Houston — its first
  expansion beyond Austin/Bay Area and the first fully driverless (no safety monitor) Texas
  markets outside Austin. Comes four days ahead of Tesla's Q1 2026 earnings report.
  [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/04/18/tesla-robotaxi-launches-dallas-houston-small-geofences/),
  [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4004/tesla-launches-unsupervised-robotaxi-service-in-dallas-and-houston)

- **2026-04-22** — Tesla's Q1 2026 shareholder update shows cumulative paid Robotaxi miles reaching
  approximately 1.7 million (nearly triple/double the prior quarter's figure); reclassifies
  Phoenix, Miami, Orlando, Tampa, and Las Vegas from a firm "1H 2026" launch commitment to a vaguer
  "preparations underway" status, effectively delaying those five markets.
  [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/04/22/tesla-seems-to-say-robotaxi-launch-will-be-pushed-back-in-5-us-cities/),
  [Not a Tesla App Q1 recap](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4031/everything-tesla-announced-during-its-q1-2026-earnings-call-summaryrecap)

- **2026-04-24** — Musk confirms on X that Tesla has begun Cybercab production at Gigafactory
  Texas.
  [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-24/musk-says-tesla-has-begun-production-of-its-cybercab-robotaxi)

- **2026-05-13** — Tracker-cited data shows Tesla's unsupervised fleet at 27 (Austin) + 5 (Dallas)
  + 6 (Houston) = 39 total vehicles.
  [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/05/13/tesla-is-rolling-out-more-unsupervised-robotaxis/)

- **2026-05-26** — Reporting describes Tesla's fleet as "shrinking, not growing": unsupervised
  fleet down to 20 total (Austin 14, Dallas 3, Houston 3); Tesla's overall active ride-hailing
  fleet (including supervised Bay Area vehicles) falls to 34, down sharply from 165 in April 2026,
  driven mainly by the Bay Area fleet collapsing from 107 to 9 active vehicles.
  [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/05/26/tesla-robotaxi-fleet-shrinking-not-growing/)

- **2026-05-28** — A new Texas law (SB 2807) requiring AV companies to register fleet counts with
  the Texas DMV takes effect. First public disclosure: Waymo has 577 registered AVs in Texas versus
  Tesla's 42 (with Avride at 317 and Nuro at 47) — the first official, regulator-sourced Texas
  fleet count for Tesla.
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/waymo-dominates-texas-autonomous-vehicle-registrations-as-tesla-trails-behind/)

- **2026-06-03** — Tesla expands its unsupervised Austin geofence to cover the entire Austin metro
  area (~245 sq mi, its fifth expansion), including Pflugerville, Manor, I-35 corridor, Gigafactory
  Texas, and Austin-Bergstrom Airport — despite an active fleet of only about 20 vehicles per
  tracker data.
  [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/06/03/tesla-expands-unsupervised-robotaxi-service-to-whole-austin-metro-area/)

- **2026-06-05** — Tesla Robotaxi LLC files a Nevada Autonomous Vehicle Network Company (AVNC)
  permit application (Docket 26-05015) covering Clark County/Las Vegas, requesting authority for up
  to 5,000 vehicles in year one. Public comment period runs through 2026-07-05; approval still
  pending as of this dataset's compilation date (2026-07-22).
  [DriveTeslaCanada](https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/tesla-nevada-robotaxi-permit-5000-vehicles-las-vegas/),
  [Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/news/news-columns/road-warrior/tesla-robotaxi-appears-to-be-gearing-up-for-vegas-launch-3841256/)

- **2026-07-03** — Tesla launches unsupervised Robotaxi service in Miami, FL — its first market
  outside Texas/California, and its first fully driverless (no safety monitor at all, from day
  one) launch in a brand-new state. Geofence covers roughly 10-14 sq mi of western Miami-Dade,
  excluding downtown/Brickell. Field reports put the visible launch-day fleet at just 2 vehicles.
  [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319711/20260704/tesla-robotaxi-arrives-miami-floridas-rain-hardest-test-yet-camera-only-fsd.htm),
  [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/07/03/tesla-robotaxi-miami-service-area-map/),
  [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/tech/tesla-robotaxi-miami-launch-comes-limits)

- **2026-07-03** — Separately, Texas statewide DMV-registered Tesla AV count is reported at ~175
  vehicles, "up by more than 100 in the past month."
  [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/07/60536701/teslas-robotaxi-land-grab-miami-added-texas-fleet-grows)

- **2026-07-21** — Tesla launches unsupervised Robotaxi service in Orlando and Tampa, FL — cities
  #6 and #7 in Tesla's active-market count (across Austin, Bay Area, Dallas, Houston, Miami,
  Orlando, Tampa), 18 days after Miami. Tesla does not disclose fleet size or whether access is
  public or invite-only in either city at launch.
  [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4470/tesla-robotaxi-launches-unsupervised-rides-in-orlando-tampa)

- **2026-07-22** — Tesla's Q2 2026 earnings call is scheduled for 5:30pm ET this date (after this
  dataset's research cutoff); expected to provide updated Robotaxi fleet/mileage figures not yet
  reflected here.
  [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4453/teslas-2026-q2-earnings-call-how-to-listen)

## Not yet reached as of 2026-07-22
- **Phoenix, AZ** — TNC (safety-driver) permit held since 2025-11-17; unsupervised/public launch
  not yet confirmed. Staging fleet (~60 Model Ys) spotted ~April 2026.
- **Las Vegas, NV** — AVNC commercial permit application pending regulatory review since
  2026-06-05; not yet launched.
