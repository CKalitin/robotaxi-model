## Notes on sourcing quality
- Several secondary aggregator/SEO-style sites (e.g., basenor.com, tesery.com, eletric-vehicles.com,
  teslahubs.com, tech-insider.org) appeared frequently in search results and were used only where
  their factual claims were corroborated by at least one primary/mainstream outlet (CNBC, Bloomberg,
  TechCrunch, Reuters-syndicated, Electrek, CleanTechnica, InsideEVs, Not a Tesla App). Numbers
  appearing solely in a single lower-tier aggregator (e.g., a "573 vehicles" Dallas/Houston figure
  from tech-insider.org) were explicitly flagged as uncorroborated and excluded from the CSV data
  files.
- Tesla's own investor-relations PDF (assets-ir.tesla.com) could not be parsed directly by the
  fetch tool used in this research; all "official" Tesla figures in this dataset are therefore
  sourced through reputable outlets that directly quote Tesla's shareholder decks/earnings calls,
  as cited above.
