# Waymo rollout timeline — official milestones

Dates are YYYY-MM-DD where a precise date was found; otherwise YYYY-MM. Every entry has an
inline source link. Company-wide ridership/mile milestones are cross-referenced in more detail
in `ridership.csv`.

## Origins
- **2009-01**: Google begins its self-driving car project (later Waymo) under Sebastian Thrun.
  https://historytimelines.co/timeline/waymo
- **2016-12**: The project spins out of Google/X as an independent Alphabet subsidiary named
  Waymo. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waymo
- **2017-04**: Waymo launches its invite-only "Early Rider Program" in metro Phoenix — safety
  driver present, ~400 participants at peak. https://medium.com/waymo/waymos-early-rider-program-one-year-in-3a788f995a9c
- **2018-05**: Fiat Chrysler completes delivery of 600 Pacifica Hybrid minivans to Waymo — one of
  the earliest quasi-official fleet-size data points (an order/delivery count, not necessarily
  all deployed at once). https://amp.cbc.ca/news/canada/windsor/waymo-wants-way-more-chrysler-pacificas-1.4686134
- **2018-12-05**: Waymo One commercial ride-hailing service launches in Phoenix — first
  commercial robotaxi service in the US, limited to vetted Early Riders, safety driver retained.
  https://techcrunch.com/2018/12/05/waymo-launches-self-driving-car-service-waymo-one

## Phoenix goes driverless
- **2020-10-08**: Waymo opens **fully driverless** (no safety driver) rides to the general public
  in the Phoenix metro area — the world's first such public commercial service.
  https://waymo.com/blog/2020/10/waymo-is-opening-its-fully-driverless-service-in-phoenix/
  (contemporaneous coverage: https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2020/10/08/waymo-driverless-rides/ ,
  https://techcrunch.com/2020/10/08/waymo-starts-to-open-driverless-ride-hailing-service-to-the-public/)

## San Francisco / California regulatory track
- **2021-08**: Waymo welcomes its first riders (Trusted Testers, safety driver on board) in San
  Francisco. https://waymo.com/blog/2021/08/welcoming-our-first-riders-in-san-francisco/
- **2022-03-30**: Waymo opens driverless robotaxi service to its own SF employees.
  https://techcrunch.com/2022/03/30/waymo-opens-driverless-robotaxi-service-to-san-francisco-employees
- **2022-11**: CPUC/permits allow Waymo to begin charging fares for driverless rides in SF
  (initial, more limited permit). https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/09/waymo-can-now-charge-for-fully-driverless-services-in-san-francisco
- **2023-08-10**: CPUC grants Waymo (and Cruise) the deployment permit to charge fares for
  fully driverless, 24/7 passenger service across San Francisco — the key "official" regulatory
  unlock for SF commercial operations.
  https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/news-and-updates/all-news/cpuc-approves-permits-for-cruise-and-waymo-to-charge-fares-for-passenger-service-in-sf-2023
  (also: https://waymo.com/blog/2023/08/waymos-next-chapter-in-san-francisco/)
- **2024-06**: Waymo removes its SF waitlist — Waymo One opens to everyone in San Francisco with
  no invite code required. https://waymo.com/blog/2024/06/waymo-one-is-now-open-to-everyone-in-san-francisco/
- **2025-03-11**: Service expands to Silicon Valley/Peninsula (Mountain View, Los Altos, Palo
  Alto, parts of Sunnyvale), ~27 sq mi. https://www.cnbc.com/2025/03/11/waymo-expands-its-robotaxi-service-in-the-san-francisco-bay-area.html
- **2025-11-22**: CPUC grants regulatory approval for Waymo to expand further across the Bay Area
  and Southern California, including freeway driving. https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/22/waymo-gets-regulatory-approval-to-expand-across-bay-area-and-southern-california/
- **2026-01-29**: Waymo approved and begins operating rider-only service to/from San Francisco
  International Airport (SFO), just ahead of Super Bowl weekend. https://waymo.com/blog/2026/01/waymo-rides-at-sfo/
  (also: https://sfstandard.com/2026/01/29/waymo-begins-operating-sfo-just-time-super-bowl/)

## Phoenix airport milestone
- **2022-11**: Trusted Tester rides begin to/from Phoenix Sky Harbor airport (safety driver on
  board). https://waymo.com/blog/2022/11/making-air-travel-more-convenient-at/
- **2023-12-14** to **2024**: Curbside rider-only pickup/dropoff at Phoenix Sky Harbor rolls out
  (initially limited hours/terminals, expanding to full 24/7), making PHX the first airport in
  the world with driverless AV curbside service.
  https://techcrunch.com/2023/12/14/waymo-launches-curbside-robotaxi-pickup-at-phoenix-airport
  https://www.skyharbor.com/about-phx/news-media/press-releases/phoenix-sky-harbor-becomes-first-airport-in-the-world-to-offer-waymo-rider-only-autonomous-vehicle-service/

## US manufacturing / fleet scaling
- **2025-05-05**: Waymo announces a Mesa, AZ manufacturing facility (with Magna) to build over
  2,000 additional I-PACE vehicles through 2026, targeting "tens of thousands" of vehicles/year
  capacity long-term; fleet described as "over 1,500 vehicles" at the time.
  https://waymo.com/blog/2025/05/scaling-our-fleet-through-us-manufacturing/

## Texas quad-launch + Orlando (first simultaneous multi-city public opening)
- **2025-11-18**: Waymo announces plans to launch in more Texas and Florida cities in 2026.
  https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/18/waymo-texas-florida-2026.html
- **2026-02-24**: Waymo opens robotaxi service to select riders simultaneously in Dallas (~50 sq
  mi), Houston (~25 sq mi), San Antonio (~60 sq mi), and Orlando (~60 sq mi) — the first time
  Waymo opened to the public in multiple cities at once, bringing total commercial metros to 10.
  https://waymo.com/blog/2026/02/dallas-houston-san-antonio-orlando/
  https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/24/waymo-opens-robotaxi-service-to-select-riders-in-4-more-us-cities.html

## Texas regulatory disclosure
- **2026-05-28**: Texas SB 2807 AV-registration law takes effect; TxDMV launches a public AV
  registry (texasavtracker.com). First-ever accurate public count: Waymo has 577 vehicles
  registered in Texas vs. far fewer for Avride (317), Nuro (47), and Tesla.
  https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/waymo-dominates-texas-autonomous-vehicle-registrations-as-tesla-trails-behind/

## Coverage-area expansion milestone
- **2026-05-13**: Waymo's total US service area passes 1,400 sq mi across 11 commercial cities
  (a 27% jump from ~1,100 sq mi), described as "larger than Rhode Island."
  https://electrek.co/2026/05/13/waymo-expands-coverage-1400-square-miles-11-cities/

## The four-city driverless push (Vegas / San Diego / Denver / Tampa)
- **2026-07-08**: Waymo announces it is preparing/beginning fully autonomous (no human at the
  wheel) operations in Las Vegas, San Diego, Denver, and Tampa — Las Vegas flips to fully
  driverless (employee-only) immediately; the other three require additional mapping/validation
  before doing the same. Company reiterates a target of 1 million paid weekly rides by end of
  2026, and states total fleet is "roughly 3,500 robotaxis" spanning "past 1,400 square miles
  across 11 US cities." https://waymo.com/blog/shorts/ro-den-lv-sd-tmpa/
  https://electrek.co/2026/07/08/waymo-driverless-las-vegas-four-new-cities/
  https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/08/waymo-starts-driverless-rides-in-san-diego-las-vegas-tampa-denver.html

## Safety recalls (NHTSA) — also function as implicit fleet-size disclosures
- **2026-05 (reported ~2026-06-21 in coverage)**: Waymo recalls approximately 3,800 robotaxis
  after some entered flooded roads at highway speeds. https://www.detroitnews.com/story/business/autos/2026/06/21/waymo-recalls-nearly-3900-robotaxis-in-u-s-for-sofware-fix/90636623007/
- **2026-06-08**: Waymo's internal Safety Board initiates a recall (filed with NHTSA) of 3,871
  fifth-generation vehicles after 13 incidents (6 in Phoenix in April, 7 in San Francisco in May)
  of robotaxis entering active freeway construction zones.
  https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/18/waymo-nhtsa-voluntary-recall-robotaxis-entered-freeway-construction-zones.html
